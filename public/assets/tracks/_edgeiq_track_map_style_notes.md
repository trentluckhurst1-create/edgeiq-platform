# EDGEiQ Track Map Style Standard

Track maps are clean schematic overlays, not detailed illustrated racecourse posters.

The benchmark is the VRC/Flemington wind tracker style:

- Simple, flat course geometry.
- Single clean course stroke.
- Minimal labels placed outside the racing line.
- Small start markers only where needed.
- No fake bias panels.
- No fake profile cards.
- No external club branding.
- No thick multi-layer course shapes.
- No hand-drawn, paint-like, or poster-style rendering.
- No decorative panels sitting over the map.

EDGEiQ track maps should feel like a professional data product:

- Dark EDGEiQ background.
- Calm green course stroke.
- White/grey labels.
- Teal used only as a subtle accent.
- Rounded line caps and joins.
- Geometric precision on paths.
- Circuit is the hero.
