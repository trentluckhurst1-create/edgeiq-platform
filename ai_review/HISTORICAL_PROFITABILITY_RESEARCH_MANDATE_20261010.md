## Proposed first gate — inventory only
Request explicit independent permission for **filename/metadata-only discovery** of historical, timestamped, pre-off win price sources and existing runner/race identity documentation; do not open data, inspect rows, join, score, fit, optimise or read sealed years under this gate. Record source name, venue/provider, timestamp semantics, odds type (fixed exchange/back/lay etc.), available years, commission and transaction-cost rules, and the presence of a documented runner/race key. All source claims must be supported by metadata, not guesses. SP/BSP are NOT valid evidence of obtainable pre-off prices.

## Subsequent gate (requires separate approval)
Only if a genuine pre-off price source with point-in-time timestamps and collision-free runner/race linkage is certified, request a separately approved fixed exploratory return calculation on the existing frozen Stage011 predictions. Predeclare bet eligibility, price time, stake sizing, commissions, scratches, dead heats, non-runners, and returns. No threshold search, staking optimisation or selection on reused development years without explicit separate authority.

## Evaluation standards
Report bet count, coverage, total staked, gross/net return, ROI, annual breakdown, drawdown and sensitivity to realistic execution assumptions. Flag small samples, data gaps and selection bias. No independent profitability claim until a genuinely unexposed, predeclared evaluation window exists.
