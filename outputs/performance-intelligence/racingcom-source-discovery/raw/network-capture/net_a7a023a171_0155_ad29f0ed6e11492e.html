Splits & Sectionals

Splits & Sectionals data displayed on Racing.com is sourced from the following race-timing technology partners: From August 2021, sectional times for race meetings at Flemington, Caulfield, The Valley & Ladbrokes Park are provided by tripleSdata and Champion Data (note: previously, Trakus technology was used at Flemington, Caulfield and The Valley). From August 2023, Cranbourne and Pakenham sectional times are also provided by tripleSdata and Champion Data (note: previously, Daily Sectionals) From September 2017, data for all other Victorian tracks is provided by Daily Sectionals; and at South Australian tracks the sectional times are sourced from Equitime.

Below each split and sectional time displayed is the horse’s position-in-running (e.g. 1st) at that point of the race.

The meeting rank 'tool-tip' feature helps to identify how the fastest individual splits & sectionals for each race, which are highlighted in green, compare to the times for the rest of that particular race meeting.
