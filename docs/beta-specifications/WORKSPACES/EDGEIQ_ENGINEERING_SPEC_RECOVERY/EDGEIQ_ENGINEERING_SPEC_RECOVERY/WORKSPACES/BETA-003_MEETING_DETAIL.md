# BETA-003 — Meeting Detail Shell
Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Answers: **What is happening at this meeting, what changed, and which race needs attention?**

## Governing rules
Inherits MASTER-001. Do not reinterpret approved hierarchy, columns, alignment, colour semantics or data ownership.


## Persistent shell
Meeting title/date/status/first/last/update.
Condition strip:
`Track | Rail | Weather | Wind | Temperature | Rain 24h | Irrigation 24h | Official Update`
Exact tabs:
`RACES | SCRATCHINGS | GEAR CHANGES | TRACK | WEATHER | RESULTS`

## State/navigation
Tab switching preserves meeting/day. Opening race preserves meeting/tab/race context. Return restores same tab and practical scroll position.

## Operational rail
Governed highlights, curated track map, notes and freshness. No unsupported notes or copied assets.

## Acceptance
One persistent shell, no placeholders, consistent width/contrast/typography.
