## Purpose
Answers: **What is the official track state, rail configuration and evidence-backed historical profile?**

## Governing rules
Inherits MASTER-001. Do not reinterpret approved hierarchy, columns, alignment, colour semantics or data ownership.


## Layout
Track Condition; Rail + full curated map; Track Details; Track Pattern Analysis; Historical Comparison; Track Notes; operational rail.

## Condition fields
`Official Rating | Inspection Time | Going Stick | Penetrometer Average | Moisture | Rain 24h | Rain 7d | Irrigation 24h | Irrigation 7d | Track Manager`

## Details
`Track Type | Surface | Circumference | Straight`
Exclude track record and class record.

## Pattern analysis
Governed plain racing language for lane/settling outcomes. Use Track Profile/Track Pattern Analysis, not Track Bias.

## Historical comparison
Context = current rating + rail.
Columns `METRIC | TODAY | LAST 3 MEETINGS | LAST 10 MEETINGS`
Allow lane/settling metrics only. Exclude average race time and average winning margin. Show sample basis.

## Acceptance
Removed records/times/margins; retained historical positional/lane comparison.
