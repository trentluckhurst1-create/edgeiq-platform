# BETA-011 — Overview Workspace
Status: LOCKED
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Answers: **What is the complete supported race picture right now?**

## Governing rules
Inherits MASTER-001. Do not reinterpret approved hierarchy, columns, alignment, colour semantics or data ownership.


## Layout
Full-width briefing:
Race Environment; MAP/Race Shape; Field Intelligence; EDGEiQ Race Read; Key Race Questions; Operational/data state.

## Content
Race conditions, field size, track/rail/weather, tempo/pressure, highest EPI and field-average context, suitability/momentum leaders, market/EDGEiQ Price context, supported positives/risks and missing-data warnings.

## Language
Evidence-led racing language, no tips or certainty.
Examples: Highest EPI in active field; 3.8 points above field average; strong current EPI but less favourable map.

## Acceptance
Full width/readable; only canonical feed; unsupported fields blank/unavailable.
