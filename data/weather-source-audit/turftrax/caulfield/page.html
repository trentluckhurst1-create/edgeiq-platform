For a typical race meeting at Caulfield up to 35 waypoint grid locations are assessed using an electromechanical GoingStick. Three 'penetration' and three 'shear' measurements, representative of forces exerted by the horses hoof when it is on contact with the ground, are taken in each grid. Volumetric soil moisture content data is also collected, meaning that 140 scientifically generated measurements contribute to each Going Report & Map produced. The process takes approximately 60 minutes and the detailed data is uploaded to a database and analysed alongsidethe live and historic weather and going data to assist with, for example, irrigation planning (if required) for a race meeting.

Live meteorological data is streamed from WeatherTrax equipment placed at 5 locations around the track, the main station is close to the 2000 meter start position. The data is logged every minute 24hrs/day and typically refreshes every five minutes on the App. Every effort is made to ensure the accuracy of this data, but values presented can differ from forecast predictions and automatically or manually recorded weather data at nearby locations. Recorded weather data can be dependent on a number of factors including; type of sensors used, methodology, height/positioning of sensing equipment and specific timing of updates.
