Track and Weather Conditions Track and Weather Conditions

The track conditions at Flemington are vital to the outcome of the races. We aim to provide you with the most up-to-date information on the track conditions and weather at Flemington. 

Please note: The track and weather conditions technology for Flemington is being upgraded. Going Stick Data is correct and updated each race day, however, wind data is turned off as part of the upgrade. We apologise for any inconvenience.

The Flemington wind tracker will provide real-time wind speed and direction at five points across the track prior to each race day. The Flemington wind tracker is the first of its kind and has been developed in conjunction with Sports Data and some of Victoria’s leading jockeys.

Weather

Live meteorological data is streamed from WeatherTrax equipment. The data is logged every minute 24hrs/day and typically refreshes every five minutes on the App. Every effort is made to ensure the accuracy of this data, but values presented can differ from forecast predictions and automatically or manually recorded weather data at nearby locations. Recorded weather data can be dependent on a number of factors including; type of sensors used, methodology, height/positioning of sensing equipment and specific timing of updates.
